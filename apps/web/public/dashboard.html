What these numbers mean

Times shown is how often a page of ours appeared in someone's Google results. Clicks is how many of them actually came to the site. Average position is where we sit in the results: 1 to 10 is the first page, and anything above 20 means almost nobody scrolls that far. Early on, position is the number that moves first — clicks follow much later.

This page only covers Google. Visitor numbers from every source live in the Vercel dashboard under Analytics.
