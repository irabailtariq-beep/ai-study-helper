What these numbers mean

Times shown = we appeared in someone's Google results. Visits = they actually clicked. The order is always the same: publish a page → Google indexes it → it starts being shown → eventually someone clicks. Each arrow takes longer than the one before, so early on the only numbers that move are the first two — and those are the ones you control.
